The Sparkliest Sweater

A story about friendship and finding your sparkle.

Gus is a beautiful Nebelung cat who is known for having the longest silver fur and fluffiest tail around.

He loves being admired and getting all of the attention from his owner. The neighborhood cats pretend to be Gus's friends, but they are secretly jealous of his beautiful fur and don't like him very much.

But one day, Gus accidentally sits right in a bowl of Friskies!

The food gets everywhere and becomes tangled in his long, fluffy fur.

Gus is heartbroken and embarrassed.

His beautiful fur is completely tangled, and no matter what he tries, the mess just won't come out.

Gus has to go to the groomer.

Sadly, his fluffy coat can't be saved, and it has to be shaved away.

Without his fluffy coat, Gus doesn't feel like himself anymore.

Even worse, the other cats tease him about his new haircut. Gus feels sad and embarrassed.

Then one day, Gus's owner comes home with a new kitten named Ralphie.

Ralphie is excited to meet his new big brother.

Ralphie instantly befriends Gus.

Gus tells Ralphie about his beautiful fur and why losing it has made him feel so sad even though he is still his parent's favorite cat.

Ralphie listens carefully and feels sorry for Gus.

Ralphie has an idea!

He sneaks into the craft room and gets to work sewing something special for his new friend.

After lots of hard work, Ralphie finishes a pretty blue sweater for Gus.

But when he looks at it, he realizes it needs something more...

Gus is special, so an ordinary sweater just won't do!

Ralphie adds colorful rhinestones, decorations, and as much sparkle as he can.

Ralphie proudly gives Gus his brand new sparkly sweater.

Gus loves it! He can't believe Ralphie made something so special just for him.

Gus realizes that what makes him special was never just his fluffy fur.

Sometimes, it takes a true friend to see your sparkle even when you feel like you're not shining. Now Gus and Ralphie share all of the Churu treats and get the most snuggles from their owner. They are the best of friends!
